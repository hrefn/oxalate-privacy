Oxalate Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

The short version

Oxalate Tracker does not collect, send or sell your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What is stored, and where

The foods you log, your daily limit, saved recipes, favorites and settings are saved only on your device. The developer has no server and no account system, and cannot see this information. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

What is not collected

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and the developer never sees your payment details. To confirm that your Apple ID owns the unlock, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and purchase status, and does not receive your food log. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Reports and exports

If you create a PDF report or export your log, the file is made on your device and shared only through the share sheet you choose. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

Not medical advice

Oxalate values are estimates from published food tables and are for general information only. Talk to your doctor or a dietitian about your own diet.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
